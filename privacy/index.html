Privacy Policy

Effective 8 November 2026

This policy explains what the RentNest app for Android and iPhone ("RentNest", "we", "us") collects when landlords, co-managers and tenants use it, why, who processes it, how long it is kept and how to delete it. Questions: hello@theaiservice.com.

Who can use RentNest

RentNest is for adults 18 or older. It is not directed at children, and we delete any account we learn belongs to a child.

What we collect

  • Account: your name, email address, phone number if you add it, the sign-in method you use (email and password, email link, Google or Apple), language and notification settings.
  • Property and tenancy records a landlord enters: property addresses, units, tenant names and contact details, lease dates, rent, deposits and late-fee settings, notes.
  • Rent records: charges, payments the landlord records (amount, date, method such as cash, check or bank transfer, reference) and the resulting ledger and receipts. The current version of RentNest does not collect rent online and never asks for card or bank account numbers.
  • Subscription status: whether a landlord has RentNest Pro, its product, renewal and trial status, which we receive from Google Play or the App Store through our subscription provider RevenueCat. We never receive your payment card details.
  • Content you add: photos (maintenance issues, inspections, receipts), documents (leases, notices, insurance), messages between landlord and tenant, maintenance updates, and typed sign-offs on inspections and documents, recorded with the time and IP address.
  • Device: a push notification token so we can send you notifications you have turned on.

What we don’t collect

  • Your location, contacts, health data or browsing history.
  • Advertising identifiers. RentNest shows no ads.
  • Analytics or crash-reporting data. The app contains no analytics or crash-reporting service.
  • Your photo library. You choose individual photos with the system picker; we receive only those.

We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising or tracking across other companies’ apps and websites.

How we use it

  • To provide RentNest: property records, rent charges and ledgers, reminders, maintenance, documents, inspections and messaging.
  • To keep accounts and data secure: authentication, rate limiting, and an audit log of financial and document actions (who, what, when and from which IP address).
  • To answer you when you contact us.

Optional AI features

Lease summaries, receipt reading, maintenance triage and notice drafts send only the text or image that feature needs to our AI provider, Anthropic, which processes it on our behalf and does not use it to train its models. Every AI result is shown to you to confirm before it changes your records. AI output is not legal advice.

Who can see your information

  • Landlords see the records for their own properties and tenants.
  • Tenants see only their own lease, charges, payments, receipts, the documents their landlord chooses to share, their maintenance requests and their messages — never other tenants’ information.

Who processes it for us

  • Supabase — hosting, database, sign-in and file storage, in the United States.
  • RevenueCat — subscription status.
  • Google and Apple — sign-in if you choose it, push notifications, and app store billing.
  • Anthropic — optional AI features, as described above.
  • An email delivery provider, to send sign-in links, invitations and notifications.

They act on our instructions and only to provide these services. We disclose information to others only where the law requires it or to protect people’s safety.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections and is encrypted at rest. Photos and documents are in private storage, reached only through short-lived links after a permission check, and database row-level security limits every landlord and tenant to their own records. You can turn on a biometric app lock.

Retention and deletion

We keep your information while your account exists. You can delete your account in the app (Settings → Delete account) or by following the steps on our account deletion page. You can export your data first.

  • Within 30 days your profile, sign-in identities, push tokens, settings, uploaded photos and documents are deleted, and messages you sent are replaced with “deleted” where the other person’s conversation must stay readable.
  • Rent charge, payment and ledger records (amounts, dates, status and the tenancy they belong to), and the audit log of financial actions, are kept for up to 7 years because the other party needs them for tax, accounting and disputes. They contain no card or bank numbers.
  • Database backups expire within a further 30 days.
  • Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play or App Store subscription; cancel it in your store account.

Your rights

You can see and export your data (Settings → Export my data), correct it in the app, delete it as described above, change notification settings, and revoke camera, photo or notification permissions in your device settings. To make any other request write to hello@theaiservice.com; we answer within 30 days. Depending on where you live (for example California, the EU or the UK) you may have further rights, including complaining to your data protection authority.

Fair housing

RentNest does not provide tenant screening or scoring, and is designed not to help anyone treat applicants or tenants differently based on protected characteristics.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page and its effective date, and tell you in the app about material changes.
