APP NAME (30 max)
RentNest: Rent & Repairs

SHORT DESCRIPTION (80 max)
Track rent, expenses, repairs and leases for 1–20 rental units.

FULL DESCRIPTION
RentNest is the simple command center for landlords with 1 to 20 units. Track rent, log expenses, handle repairs and keep leases organized, all from your phone.

TRACK RENT
• Monthly rent charges generated for every tenant.
• Record payments you receive by cash, check, bank transfer or Zelle in seconds.
• A clear ledger and receipt for every tenant, and overdue alerts.

SEE EVERYTHING AT A GLANCE
• Rent collected vs. expected this month.
• Overdue tenants, open repair requests and upcoming lease expirations.

HANDLE REPAIRS
• Tenants submit requests with photos.
• Emergency keywords show a call-for-help banner right away.
• Schedule, assign a vendor and log the cost, and it becomes an expense automatically.

TRACK EXPENSES
• Snap receipts and categorize spending per property.
• Profit & loss per property and an export for your accountant.

LEASES, DOCUMENTS AND INSPECTIONS
• Private document storage per property, unit and tenant.
• Room-by-room move-in and move-out inspections with photos and sign-off.
• Notice templates (templates only, not legal advice).

MESSAGING
• Message each tenant in the app.

RentNest is free: tenants use it free, and landlords can manage up to 20 units at no cost.
