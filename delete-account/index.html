Delete your RentNest account

For RentNest on Android and iPhone

In the app

  1. Open RentNest and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings → Delete account.
  3. Optionally tap Export my data first.
  4. Confirm by typing DELETE. You are signed out straight away.

Without the app

Email hello@theaiservice.com with the subject “Delete my RentNest account”, from the email address you sign in with. We confirm the request with you and complete it within 30 days.

What is deleted

Within 30 days: your profile, sign-in identities, push tokens, settings, photos and documents you uploaded, and — for a landlord — properties, units and tenant contact records not needed for the retained records below. Messages you sent are replaced with “deleted” where the other person’s conversation must stay readable.

What is kept, and for how long

Record Kept for Why
Rent charges, recorded payments and ledger entries (amounts, dates, status) Up to 7 years The landlord and tenant need them for tax, accounting and disputes
Audit log of financial and document actions Up to 7 years Security and dispute evidence
Record that the deletion was done Up to 7 years Proof of deletion

Database backups expire within a further 30 days. These records contain no card or bank numbers.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel RentNest Pro. Cancel it in Google Play (Payments & subscriptions) or in your Apple account settings.
